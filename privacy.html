PRIVACY / INQUIRIES

Respecting the information you share.

Draft notice — operator details must be completed before public deployment. Synthetic is an early-stage robotics validation initiative. This website is intended to gather voluntary business inquiries, not confidential engineering files.

What an inquiry collects

When you submit the contact form, it may collect your name, work email address, organisation (if provided), project stage and the description you choose to share. Do not submit passwords, personal sensitive information, source code, unreleased designs or other trade secrets through this form.

How inquiry information is used

The site operator intends to use inquiry details to respond, discuss potential engineering work, and follow up on the same inquiry. We do not intend to sell inquiry details or use them for unrelated marketing. The form is designed to use Netlify Forms upon deployment; the hosting provider may process submitted data as part of providing the service.

Retention and rights

Inquiries should be reviewed and deleted when no longer needed for the conversation, unless a legitimate business or legal reason requires longer retention. You may request access, correction or deletion by submitting a privacy-related request through the site's inquiry form. A direct privacy contact address and full legal operator identity must be added before collecting live submissions.

Analytics and cookies

This first version contains no third-party analytics scripts, advertising trackers or optional cookie banners. Hosting infrastructure may process standard request and security logs. If analytics or additional services are added, this notice must be updated first.

Important boundary

Information here explains the intended initial workflow and must be reviewed for the laws applicable to the website operator and visitors before production publication. Last drafted: 30 October 2026.
