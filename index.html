01 / THE PROBLEM

What works in the lab can fail at the edges.

Robotic systems inherit hidden assumptions about the world. Those assumptions become liabilities when hardware, software and operating conditions evolve.

01 ↗

Distribution shift

Lighting, clutter, object poses and material properties move outside familiar test conditions.

THE WORLD CHANGES
02 ⌁

Physical variability

Payload, friction, tolerance, wear and compliance alter how a commanded action unfolds.

PHYSICS INTERVENES
03 ≋

Integration fragility

Control latency, sensor timing, interfaces and recovery behaviour expose system-level weaknesses.

SYSTEMS INTERACT
04 ▧

Validation debt

Unversioned tests and missing evidence make future changes costly to assess and difficult to trust.

UNCERTAINTY COMPOUNDS
✳

We don't promise to eliminate failures. We help teams find important ones earlier, quantify what is known and make uncertainty visible.

03 / OUR APPROACH

From impressive behaviour
to defensible evidence.

Robustness is not one score. It is a repeatable process for asking better questions about a system's limits.

01

Define the operating envelope

Specify tasks, environments, system versions, acceptance criteria and what the evidence must support.

BOUNDARY
02

Introduce controlled variation

Test loads, poses, timing, contact conditions and other factors that could matter in the intended deployment.

PERTURBATION
03

Measure behaviour and failure

Collect repeatable trials, classify failure modes, record interventions and preserve test configuration.

MEASUREMENT
04

Make the next decision clearer

Document supported conclusions, residual uncertainty and the next tests or design changes worth prioritising.

04 / DESIGN FOR CHANGE

The most expensive validation gap may be the one you inherit later.

As robots move from prototype to fleet, untested assumptions and undocumented interfaces become harder to untangle. Build the evaluation discipline early enough that each new version is easier to assess.
